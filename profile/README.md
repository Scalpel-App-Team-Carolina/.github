# Scalpel · App Team Carolina

## Now building: Scalpel

Scalpel is a tool for medical residents to track their surgeries and overall performance. It gives residents a clear record of their work so they can advocate for themselves with physicians and residency programs, and it shows whether they are on track to meet all of their yearly residency requirements.

## Previously: Cupboard

Cupboard is an inventory-tracking app for Carolina Cupboard, UNC's food insecurity nonprofit. Workers who previously tracked inventory by hand now use Cupboard to streamline stock management and reduce waste.
